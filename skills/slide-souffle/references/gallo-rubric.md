# Gallo audit rubric

Score scale for every principle: **1** works against the principle · **2** some evidence,
substantial problems · **3** present, not distinctive · **4** clear and effective ·
**5** exceptional and memorable.

Weight the principles equally. Deviate only when the talk's purpose makes a principle more
or less relevant, and state the change.

## 1. Unleash the Master Within

- **Deck evidence:** clear thesis, point of view, stakes, specificity, first-hand insight.
- **Not assessable from slides:** enthusiasm, vocal energy, emotional authenticity.
- **High:** a distinct point of view and a clear reason why the subject matters.
- **Low:** information dump, no thesis, no stakes, interchangeable content.

## 2. Master the Art of Storytelling

- **Deck evidence:** characters, conflict, before/after, journey, concrete incidents, callbacks.
- **High:** stories advance the argument.
- **Low:** chronology without narrative, abstract claims only, anecdote detached from the thesis.

## 3. Have a Conversation

- **Deck evidence:** concise phrasing, audience-directed language, few paragraphs.
- **Partially assessable:** voice, pacing, gestures, rehearsal, eye contact.
- **High:** slides support spoken explanation instead of duplicating it.
- **Low:** dense text the presenter has to read out.

## 4. Teach Me Something New

- **Deck evidence:** explicit insight, surprising synthesis, novel framing, useful model.
- **High:** the audience can say what it learned.
- **Low:** familiar material repackaged without a distinct insight.

## 5. Deliver Jaw-Dropping Moments

- **Deck evidence:** staged reveal, demo setup, surprising number, prop cue, dramatic before/after.
- **High:** at least one peak, deliberately prepared and tied to the thesis.
- **Low:** flat rhythm without a high point.
- Never demand spectacle for its own sake.

## 6. Lighten Up

- **Deck evidence:** playful examples, visual wit, light phrasing, contrast in tone.
- **Partially assessable:** timing and delivery decide whether humour works.
- **High:** levity supports the content.
- **Low:** relentlessly dry where the context permits warmth.
- Never penalize a serious talk where humour is inappropriate.

## 7. Stick to the 18-Minute Rule

- **Deck evidence:** disciplined scope, prioritization, slide density, appendix use, no detours.
- **Duration known:** judge against the real timebox, not against 18 minutes.
- **Duration unknown:** judge density only and mark the principle partially assessable.
- **High:** focused argument with deliberate exclusions.
- **Low:** bloated scope, redundant sections, competing ideas.

## 8. Paint a Mental Picture with Multisensory Experiences

- **Deck evidence:** explanatory visuals, diagrams, video or audio cues, demos, objects, vivid examples.
- **High:** media deepen understanding or memory.
- **Low:** concepts stay abstract or text-only.
- Never reward decorative imagery without explanatory value.

## 9. Stay in Your Lane

- **Deck evidence:** first-hand examples, clear scope of authority, acknowledged limits, consistent voice.
- **Partially assessable:** personal authenticity.
- **High:** claims are grounded and the speaker's perspective has a reason to exist.
- **Low:** borrowed authority, unexplained certainty, claims beyond shown expertise.
